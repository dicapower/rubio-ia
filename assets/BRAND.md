# Brand

Rubio-IA and noah are two open projects that work together, and each has a dog for a logo.

| Project | Logo | Dog |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rubio-IA | `rubio.svg` (this repo: `logo.svg`) | Rubio, an Australian shepherd |
| noah | `noah.svg` (this repo: `logo.svg`) | Noah, a rottweiler |
| Both | `social-preview.png` | Rubio, Roma and Noah side by side |

Roma, a pit bull, appears only in `social-preview.svg` / `social-preview.png` (also `roma.svg`, `roma.png`). She is decoration, not a mascot of either project.

Rules: do not recolour or stretch the logos, do not add text inside the square, and do not recompose the shared image by hand. GitHub only accepts the social preview through Settings → General → Social preview, so it is uploaded manually.

Colours: paper `#fbf8f2`, ink `#1c1c21`, muted `#4b5563`; Rubio gold `#c98a4b` on lavender `#e6defa`; noah rust `#b8692f` on cream `#f3ead7`; Roma slate `#566070` on ice blue `#d9e6f2`.
